Getting Started

What is an LLM wiki?

Knowledge compiled once into linked pages, instead of being worked out again every time you ask.

Key ideas

  • A wiki you keep. Answers worth keeping become pages you can read and build on.
  • Written by you or an AI agent. A language model (LLM) can draft and maintain pages on your computer. Wiki LLM on iPhone and iPad shows and organises them.
  • Plain files. Every page is an ordinary HTML file in a folder you control.
Good to knowThe iPhone and iPad app does not contain an AI model and does not upload your pages. It reads the files in your library.

Check yourself

Where do your pages live?

Answer: In a folder on your device, as plain HTML files that any browser can open.
